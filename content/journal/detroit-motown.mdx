Confession: I didn't really know what to expect from Detroit, and I'm a little embarrassed about that, because Detroit slaps. We found a diner where the waitress called us both "baby" in a way that somehow meant nothing and everything at once. Motown came out of a speaker held together with tape. And the borderline religious experience of the coney dogs... Oh my God!

Then we went to Hitsville — the Motown Museum — and I learned a critical fact about Jacob Monroe.

The man cannot dance.

He has many gifts — like being 1000% fine. He can parallel park on the first try. He stays calm when I am being, by all accounts, so extra. But there's this little spot where you can try the old Temptations steps, and Jacob attempted them, and he looked like a very handsome scarecrow being attacked by bees. *I have never loved anyone this much.* I filmed it. I will treasure it. He made me promise not to post it, so it's going in the vault, right next to the snow globe.

Texted the clip to Abby anyway. She sent back nine crying-laughing emojis and two words: "marry him."

Working on it, Ab. Slowly. Working on it.

— E
